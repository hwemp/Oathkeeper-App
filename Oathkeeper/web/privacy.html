Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the standalone Oathkeeper mobile app.

Data on your device

Character statistics, inventory, notes and imported sheet text are stored locally. PDFs are processed on your device. The local character tools work without an account and do not include imported documents in optional support reports. Community features require an account and completed age review.

Sharing and backups

Backup export creates a JSON file. You choose its destination using the operating system’s share controls. That destination’s own privacy policy applies. Your device may include app data in operating-system backups, depending on your settings.

Collection and tracking

The app includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. A Store is planned for future in-app purchases. It is currently empty, with no products or checkout enabled. App stores and the operating system may process download, diagnostic and crash information under their own policies.

Deletion

Use Settings → Reset character to erase the stored character and restore the starting profile. You can also clear app data or uninstall. Separately exported files and device backups must be deleted separately. Temporary shared files are stored in the app cache and can be removed by the operating system or by clearing app data.

Contact

Send information, reports, bugs, privacy questions or safety concerns to ecclesyus@gmail.com. You can also copy a redacted Support report for independent review. External assistance does not authorize contact approvals.

Optional in-app community

Community screens are bundled inside the app. Once the online service is available, signing in sends your username and password to that service over HTTPS. Passwords are not saved in character backups. An HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in; use Community → Account → Sign out to end the session.

Posts, comments, group membership, friend requests, blocks, messages, reports, and files that you choose to share are stored by the community service. Community content follows age-review and approved-contact rules; private group content additionally requires membership, and direct messages are restricted to permitted participants. Reports share the reported content and reason with moderators. Local characters and imported documents are never automatically uploaded.

Delete your own posts and messages through community controls. Post deletion also removes its attached files. Account deletion and password recovery are not available in this testing version. Export your local character backup before closing your account; automatic email delivery of characters is not configured. Uninstalling the app does not delete community content. Online service and durable file storage must be configured before shared use.

Optional Support diagnosis

Support keeps a small diagnostic history on your device. Email reports are drafts you review and send. Optional Repair Review sends your redacted report to the configured repair service for automated analysis using an external provider (OpenAI). Your character, journal and DM notes are not automatically attached. Review reports before sending. Automatic diagnosis is off until enabled. Repairs require separate owner approval before a draft pull request can be created; nothing is automatically installed.

Full backups include private DM notes and saved roll history. Share them only with people allowed to read those details.

Birthdate and child safety

Community signup requires a real birthdate. It is stored privately by the community service for age review and is not included in character backups or shown to other members. Entry alone is not proof of identity. Contact stays locked until a moderator completes age review, including for existing accounts.

Members under 18 cannot contact adults aged 18 or older unless a family or existing-friend exception has been approved. Both people must independently contact ecclesyus@gmail.com through the verified support process, identifying both usernames and the request reference. A moderator must verify both independent contacts before approving. Friend requests, shared groups, email drafts or automated responses never grant approval. Either person can withdraw an exception; exceptions expire and remain subject to blocking.

If someone pressures you to keep secrets, send private images or personal details, or move off the app, stop contact, block and report them, and tell a trusted adult. Do not send passwords or identity documents in ordinary support reports. Contact support for the private age-review process.
