Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Applies to the standalone Oathkeeper mobile app.

Data on your device

Character statistics, inventory, notes and imported sheet text are stored locally. PDFs are processed on your device. The app does not operate a character-data server, require an account, or send imported documents to an AI service.

Sharing and backups

Backup export creates a JSON file. You choose its destination using the operating system’s share controls. That destination’s own privacy policy applies. Your device may include app data in operating-system backups, depending on your settings.

Collection and tracking

The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. App stores and the operating system may process download, diagnostic and crash information under their own policies.

Deletion

Use Settings → Reset character to erase the stored character and restore the starting profile. You can also clear app data or uninstall. Separately exported files and device backups must be deleted separately. Temporary shared files are stored in the app cache and can be removed by the operating system or by clearing app data.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact provided on this app’s store listing.

Optional online community

The Community tab opens your chosen HTTPS community in a browser window. Its operator handles accounts, friends, messages, reports, and shared files under that community’s privacy terms. Your offline character is not automatically uploaded. The chosen address is stored on this device; Forget address clears it. Sign out within the community to end its login session.
