Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the standalone Oathkeeper mobile app.

Data on your device

Character statistics, inventory, notes and imported sheet text are stored locally. PDFs are processed on your device. The app does not operate a character-data server, require an account, or send imported documents to an AI service.

Sharing and backups

Backup export creates a JSON file. You choose its destination using the operating system’s share controls. That destination’s own privacy policy applies. Your device may include app data in operating-system backups, depending on your settings.

Collection and tracking

The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. App stores and the operating system may process download, diagnostic and crash information under their own policies.

Deletion

Use Settings → Reset character to erase the stored character and restore the starting profile. You can also clear app data or uninstall. Separately exported files and device backups must be deleted separately. Temporary shared files are stored in the app cache and can be removed by the operating system or by clearing app data.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email ecclesyus@gmail.com.

Optional in-app community

Community screens are bundled inside the app. Once the online service is available, signing in sends your username and password to that service over HTTPS. Passwords are not saved in character backups. An HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in; use Community → Account → Sign out to end the session.

Posts, comments, group membership, friend requests, blocks, messages, reports, and files that you choose to share are stored by the community service. Public posts are visible to everyone; private group content is restricted to members and direct messages to the participants. Reports share the reported content and reason with moderators. Local characters and imported documents are never automatically uploaded.

Delete your own posts and messages through community controls. Post deletion also removes its attached files. Account deletion and password recovery are not available in this testing version. Uninstalling the app does not delete community content. Online service and durable file storage must be configured before shared use.

Optional Support diagnosis

Support keeps a small diagnostic history on your device. Email reports are drafts you review and send. Optional AI diagnosis sends your redacted report to the repair service you configure and its OpenAI API provider. Your character, journal and DM notes are not automatically attached. Review reports before sending. Automatic diagnosis is off until enabled. Repairs require separate owner approval before a draft pull request can be created; nothing is automatically installed.

Full backups include private DM notes and saved roll history. Share them only with people allowed to read those details.
