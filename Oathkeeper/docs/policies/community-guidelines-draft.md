# Oathkeeper — Community Standards / Child Safety (PRE-PUBLICATION DRAFT)
**Community remains disabled until staffed moderation, verified safeguards, deletion, complaints handling and backend audits are complete.** This draft is not an assertion that such services are already operating.

## No abuse or exploitation
Do not threaten, harass, discriminate, bully, impersonate, extort, groom, exploit, or endanger anyone. Child sexual abuse and exploitation, related material, facilitation, solicitation, or sharing are strictly prohibited. Do not request or share intimate images of minors. Do not pressure anyone to move to another service, conceal interactions or share private credentials. Flag suspected child endangerment immediately to the trained safety team and, where required, competent authorities. Preserve evidence only as legally required with restricted access; do not encourage redistribution.

## Content and intellectual property
Share only content you own or are permitted to share. Do not post pirated D&D books, copied D&D Beyond character art, protected rulebook pages or unlicensed maps. Report suspected copyright/trademark misuse using the published takedown contact. Document notice, counter-notice and repeat-infringer workflows as required by applicable law before hosting user content.

## Reports, blocks and moderation
Users must be able to report posts, users, messages and uploads, block abusive accounts, and receive an acknowledgement. Reports must be routed to trained reviewers and handled promptly under documented service targets; urgent child-safety allegations require priority escalation. Include filtering for prohibited content before publication, sanctions, repeat-offender review, an appeals channel, logging and safeguards against retaliatory reports. Do not promise instant or comprehensive human monitoring without staffing it.

## Age and contacts
Do not open community, direct messages, groups or file transfer to minors until lawfully verified age/guardian procedures and backend enforcement are independently evaluated. Self-declared DOB, two emails, two SMS texts or a claimed family relationship do NOT alone establish verified guardian consent. Server-side policy must enforce restrictions on every API endpoint and stored file, not just buttons. Minors' data must be minimized and protected according to jurisdiction.

## Privacy and retention
Limit reporter/moderator access to need-to-know, use encryption and access logs, publish retention/deletion rules, prevent public disclosure of birthdates and safety evidence, and support account deletion. App uninstall is not deletion of server content.

## Publication requirements
- Published, HTTPS-accessible rules and contact information
- In-app reporting and blocking mechanisms demonstrated on both platforms
- Staffing, appeals, escalation, moderation queue and response records tested
- Sensitive media screening and attachment validation tested
- Age assurance, lawful guardian consent and cross-age prevention independently reviewed
- Backend authentication/authorization and data deletion demonstrated on a staging instance
- Appropriate store declarations and lawful incident handling completed
